# Company Research

External queries may contain company and role information only. Never send PersonalWiki text, candidate facts, contact details, resume content, or interview answers to research tools.

Prefer official company sources, then other primary sources, then reputable secondary sources. Label anecdotal material. Save bounded source excerpts under the opportunity `sources/` folder and register every URL actually used with `uv run interviewwiki research register`.

Separate captured excerpts from generated synthesis, distinguish direct claims from inference, capture publication/retrieval context, and preserve conflicting evidence. Do not register one URL for claims synthesized from other pages. Research describes the opportunity; it never proves a candidate qualification.
